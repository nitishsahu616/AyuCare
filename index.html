Designated Emergency Contacts:

    Stroke (F.A.S.T.)

    • Face: Ask person to smile. Does one side droop?
    • Arms: Raise both arms. Does one drift downward?
    • Speech: Slurred or strange phrasing?
    • Time: Call 108 / 112 instantly if any sign matches.

    Severe Burns

    • Cool with cool running tap water for 15-20 minutes.
    • Do NOT apply ice, toothpaste, or turmeric.
    • Cover loosely with a sterile, non-adherent dressing.
    • Seek immediate burn unit care for blisters on face/hands.

    Choking (Heimlich)

    • Stand behind person, wrap arms around their waist.
    • Make a fist above their navel; grasp with other hand.
    • Deliver rapid upward and inward abdominal thrusts.
    • If patient turns unresponsive, begin CPR immediately.
